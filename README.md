My engineering work covers the embedded development cycle—from **schematic and PCB design to firmware development, hardware bring-up, debugging, validation and production support**.

* Embedded C and bare-metal firmware
* STM32, PIC, AVR and ESP32
* Peripheral drivers and low-level MCU development
* GPIO, ADC, PWM, timers, interrupts and DMA
* UART, SPI, I²C, CAN and USB
* PCB design and multilayer board development
* Altium Designer
* Motor control and BLDC systems
* Power electronics and inverter systems
* Hardware bring-up and debugging
* Reusable embedded software architecture

## Engineering Approach

I focus on understanding embedded systems at both the **hardware and software levels**.

```text
System Requirements
        ↓
Circuit & Schematic Design
        ↓
PCB Development
        ↓
Hardware Bring-up
        ↓
Low-Level Firmware
        ↓
Peripheral & Interface Integration
        ↓
Debugging & Validation
        ↓
System-Level Reliability
```

The objective is to develop embedded systems that are **well-structured, testable, maintainable and practical for real hardware**.
